# The Notebook

![The Notebook poster](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQrXnxP5sszhMeSxkLQRSncWE4izWO0XFp9Dy5ihTUekK1RFg8HqTv6j2fc&s=10)

**The Notebook** (2004) is my *favorite* romance drama. It's emotional, beautifully shot, and I can rewatch it again and again.

---

## Quick Facts

- **Director:** Nick Cassavetes
- **Released:** 2004
- **Based on:** the novel by Nicholas Sparks
- **Genre:** Romance / Drama
- **Setting:** South Carolina, 1940s
  - Young love story
  - Present-day nursing home

## Main Characters

| Character   | Played By      | Description                   |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| Noah        | Ryan Gosling   | Hardworking, hopeful romantic |
| Allie       | Rachel McAdams | Free-spirited, determined     |
| Older Noah  | James Garner   | Reads the story every day     |
| Older Allie | Gena Rowlands  | The heart of the story        |

## My Top 3 Scenes

1. The rainy boat scene on the lake

## Why I Love It
